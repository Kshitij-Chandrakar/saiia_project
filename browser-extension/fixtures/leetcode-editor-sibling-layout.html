Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock III

Given an integer array prices where prices[i] is the price of a given stock on the ith day, return the maximum profit.

Example 1

Input: prices = [3,3,5,0,0,3,1,4]

Output: 6

Explanation: Buy on day 4 and sell on day 6, then buy on day 7 and sell on day 8.

Example 2

Input: prices = [1,2,3,4,5]

Output: 4

Explanation: Buy on day 1 and sell on day 5.

Output: 0
