Transcript TXT/MD downloads, permanent transcript-only deletion, resume deletion and job-context deletion exist. They do not constitute complete account erasure. Resume deletion leaves a row and potentially sensitive extraction/profile fields. Account deletion has a preparation/access barrier, not a purge worker. ## Account deletion with unresolved Razorpay cancellation

Proposed workflow only:

1. Verify subject/recent reauth, show affected data and immediate loss of product access, retained categories/device limits, and current cancellation uncertainty. Offer export before confirmation. Keeping transcripts/resumes is not necessary to manage billing.
2. Atomically record deletion/access barrier, audit and minimal billing-obligation linkage; fence Auto/generation and invalidate handoffs/queued content writes. ## Proposed technical contracts

Names/routes here are design candidates, not executable schemas or a requirement to implement every illustrative roadmap route.

- Privacy request: subject, type/scope, policy version, revision/state, confirmation/reauth safe reference, timestamps, exception summaries and idempotency identity. Tasks: owned opaque resource IDs, restricted object keys, expected generation, attempt/lease/fence, safe error and verified-absence checkpoint. No captured content or tokens; even paths/filenames can be personal.
- Confirmation commits request, barrier, success audit and outbox together. Shared account lock order/generation fence covers uploads, activation, notes/chat, answers, exports and worker commits. Storage/provider operations are not distributed transactions: durable retry and verification handle uncertain boundaries.
- Owner APIs: request/status/download export; preview/confirm/status deletion; separate resource/session/history/notes/thread/settings actions. Keep current resource routes stable. Server derives subject from JWT; admin delegation uses separate permission-checked APIs and the same lifecycle service.
- Export: versioned allowlist, recorded snapshot cutoff/generation, no SELECT * or arbitrary bucket walk; recheck ownership/restriction on build finalization and each download. Delete wins over export publication; revoke download authorization and cleanup artifacts. Validate nested sources/diagrams/file ownership and safe archive paths.
- Before enabling purge, derive full FK/dependency graph from ordered migrations, including indirect session children and JSON/result payloads, not just direct Auth FKs. Require new user-linked tables to register export/cleanup treatment. Separate personal payload from immutable accounting envelope.
- Restore suppression journal must survive old backups, be access-minimized and retained for every possible restore/callback window. Stable UUID/HMAC remains pseudonymous. Missing journal on restore fails closed; no finite marker expiry promise while replay sources are unbounded.
