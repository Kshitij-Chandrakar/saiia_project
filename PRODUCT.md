# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students, freshers, early-career developers, career switchers, and active job candidates who need real-time interview support during remote technical, behavioral, and HR interview sessions.

## Product Purpose
SAIIA is a desktop AI interview assistant that helps candidates answer interview questions in real time using microphone input, user-triggered screen reading, saved profile context, resume grounding, and a private Electron overlay. Success means the app stays fast, discreet, truthful to the candidate's background, and dependable during live interview moments.

## Brand Personality
Focused, discreet, capable.

SAIIA should feel like a calm desktop assistant, not a loud dashboard. It should project confidence, speed, and clarity while staying light enough to trust during a high-pressure interview.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI dashboards with bright gradients, oversized cards, and marketing-style chrome
- Document-chat products that feel like general-purpose copilots instead of interview tools
- Noisy gamer-style overlays or neon hacker visuals
- Overbuilt enterprise admin panels that bury the live interview workflow

## Design Principles
- Keep the live interview flow immediate, readable, and interruption-free
- Make the product feel like a desktop assistant first, not a web app transplanted into Electron
- Show private context and diagnostics clearly without overwhelming the user
- Support truthful, grounded answers rather than theatrical AI behavior
- Polish the runtime experience without destabilizing the proven backend flow

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Use readable contrast, restrained motion, clear status/error messaging, and layouts that remain usable in compact window sizes. Support reduced-motion friendly transitions and avoid visual clutter that raises cognitive load during live interviews.
